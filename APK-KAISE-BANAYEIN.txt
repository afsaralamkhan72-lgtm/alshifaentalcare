ANDROID APK (aik hi app: Patient + Staff)

Push ke baad (GitHub website par, apni repo mein):
1. "Actions" tab kholein.
2. Left side par "Android APK banao" chunein.
3. "Run workflow" dabayein (hara button), phir dobara "Run workflow".
4. 5-8 minute intezar karein. Hara tick aaye to us run par click karein.
5. Neeche "Artifacts" mein "al-shifa-apk" par click karke zip download karein. Is ke andar app-debug.apk hai.
6. Wo APK WhatsApp se bhejein / phone mein copy karein. Install karte waqt "Unknown source" ki ijazat dein.

Pehli screen par Patient ke liye "Mera record dekhein" aur "Appointment lein" hain.
Staff login chhupa hua hai: logo par 5 baar jaldi jaldi tap karein.
App aap ki live website kholti hai, isliye website badalne par APK dobara banane ki zaroorat nahi.
Pehli baar GPS wali hazri par phone location ki ijazat maangega: "Allow" dein.
